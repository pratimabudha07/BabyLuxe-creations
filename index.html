The Botanical Index

Six ingredients. Infinite tenderness.

01 / Floral Essence

Mogra (Jasmine sambac)

A single distilled drop of mogra perfumes the blend with a soft, lullaby-like sweetness — the same flower mothers tuck into a baby's pillow at dusk.

Benefit

Calms the senses and aids restful sleep.

02 / Carrier Oil

Cold-pressed Almond

Rich in vitamin E and oleic acid, our sweet almond oil is pressed within 48 hours of harvest to retain its luminous, gentle warmth.

Benefit

Nourishes and softens delicate newborn skin.

03 / Petal Infusion

Calendula Marigold

Sun-steeped petals lend a golden hue and centuries of traditional use for tender, irritated skin.

Benefit

Soothes redness, dryness and nappy rash.

04 / Ayurvedic Root

Ashwagandha

Grounded in our grandmothers' nightly malish ritual — a warming infusion to comfort little limbs after bath.

Benefit

Supports muscle relaxation and deeper sleep.

05 / Floral Water

Rose Hydrosol

A gentle distillation of Damask rose, naturally pH-balanced for the most delicate of skins.

Benefit

Hydrates, refreshes and balances skin.

06 / Tree Resin

Sandalwood Mist

A whisper of Mysore sandalwood — grounding, comforting, and the gentlest of natural antimicrobials.

Benefit

Naturally protective and gently aromatic.

Why it matters

Benefits that linger long after bath time.

  • Deep, gentle nourishment

    Cold-pressed oils penetrate without clogging pores, locking in moisture for hours.

  • A calmer bedtime ritual

    Mogra and sandalwood naturally encourage longer, more restful sleep cycles.

  • Soothes sensitive skin

    Calendula and rose hydrosol comfort eczema-prone, dry or rash-irritated skin.

  • No synthetic fragrance

    Aroma comes only from real botanicals — no phthalates, no parabens, no surprises.

  • A keepsake of memory

    Fragrance is the strongest memory-maker. We help you write your baby's first.

Safety Notes

Tested with care, used with kindness.

A few thoughtful notes to keep your little one safe and comfortable:

  1. 1
    Patch test first

    Apply a small amount on the inner forearm and wait 24 hours before regular use.

  2. 2
    Avoid eyes & mouth

    For external use only. Keep away from eyes, mouth and any broken skin.

  3. 3
    From day one — gently

    Safe for newborns when used sparingly. For under 3 months, dilute further with plain almond oil.

  4. 4
    Store with love

    Keep in a cool, dark place. Natural oils last best below 25°C, away from sunlight.

  5. 5
    Allergy aware

    Contains nut-derived oils. Please consult your paediatrician if your baby has known allergies.

  6. 6
    Use within 6 months

    Free from preservatives — best enjoyed fresh, like everything truly natural.

Born from a lullaby,

Today, we are an atelier of 40 artisans across Rajasthan, Bengal and Tamil Nadu, weaving stories that little hands will hold long after they grow.
